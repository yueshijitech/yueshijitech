Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What services do you offer?

We provide comprehensive digital solutions including mobile app development (iOS/Android), network technology R&D, e-commerce solutions, mini-program development, digital marketing, new media operations, advertising design, technical consulting, and product sales (computer hardware, software, digital products, home goods, daily necessities).

Where can I download your apps?

Our mobile applications are available on both Google Play and App Store. Search for "Yiyutuo" or visit our services page for direct links to our enterprise management applications.

Do you offer custom development?

Yes, we provide custom mobile app development, mini-program development, and tailored enterprise solutions. Contact us at wangyu@yiyutuo.com for enterprise inquiries.

What regions do you serve?

We serve clients in 30+ countries worldwide. Our team can provide support in multiple languages and cater to various international market requirements.

How can I become a partner?

We welcome partnership inquiries from technology companies, agencies, and enterprises. Please reach out to our business development team at wangyu@yiyutuo.com to discuss collaboration opportunities.

What is your response time?

We typically respond to inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent technical issues, our support team is available 24/7 for critical problems affecting your operations.
