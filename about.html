Our Story

Wuxi Yiyutuo Network Technology Co., Ltd. was founded with a clear vision: to empower businesses and individuals through cutting-edge digital solutions. From our headquarters in Wuxi, China, we have grown into a global technology company serving clients across 30+ countries.

Our diverse portfolio of mobile applications, available on both Google Play and App Store, serves millions of users worldwide. We specialize in network technology R&D, internet data services, e-commerce solutions, mini-program development, enterprise promotion, new media operations, advertising design, business consulting, and digital product sales.

Our team of 100+ professionals brings together expertise in software development, design, marketing, and business strategy, enabling us to deliver comprehensive solutions that drive real results.
